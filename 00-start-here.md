# Reviewing our incident and mitigation coding

**Research draft · Updated October 23, 2026.**

We are developing a way to turn AI incident accounts into structured, evidence-linked descriptions, and to connect those descriptions to existing risk frameworks and proposed mitigations. The central question is whether the taxonomy makes coding more consistent, inspectable and useful without claiming more than the sources establish.

The work includes 1,567 AIID development cases, all subsequently included in a 1,597-record full-text exercise. Early findings describe that baseline. A separate 116-entry cohort supports planned cross-model comparison and independent human validation; its model answers and results are withheld here.
